Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026. This policy covers Earnings Calendar, a personal tool used only by its owner (about the app).

What the app can access

The app asks Google for a single permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.app.created. It lets the app create one calendar ("Earnings") and add, change and remove events in calendars the app itself created. It cannot see or change any other calendar, and it has no access to email, contacts, files or any other Google data.

What the app stores

The sign-in token Google issues and the id of the calendar the app created are kept on the owner's own computer. The events it writes contain only public company information: names, earnings dates and times, and conference-call details.

Sharing

Data goes only between the owner's computer and the Google Calendar API. It is not sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising or analytics. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The Earnings calendar can be deleted in Google Calendar's settings.
